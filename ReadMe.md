# AI Smart Study Planner

An AI Powered Weekly Study Schedule Generator for University Students

Course: CSE 4104 - Software Development III
Team Name: CSE4104-7E-T06 | Section: 7E

## Project Description

AI Smart Study Planner is a web application that helps university students organize their subjects, assignments and exam dates. Using Google Gemini AI, it analyzes the student's workload, subject difficulty, deadlines and available daily study time to generate a personalized 7-day study schedule with short explanations for its suggestions. Unlike a basic to-do list, it helps students decide what to study, when to study it and how to distribute preparation time across subjects.

## Objectives

- Let students organize their subjects, assignments and exam dates in one place.
- Generate personalized 7-day study schedules with Google Gemini AI, based on deadlines, difficulty, estimated hours and daily available study time.
- Give short AI explanations so students understand why the schedule is arranged the way it is.
- Help students track progress through a dashboard and study session completion tracking.
- Let students save, review and reuse past study plans.

## Features

- **Authentication**: register, log in and log out (JWT + bcrypt)
- **Subject management**: add, edit and delete subjects with difficulty levels (Easy / Medium / Hard)
- **Assignment & exam management**: deadline, estimated hours and status for each task
- **Academic progress dashboard**: upcoming deadlines, completed vs pending tasks, per-subject progress
- **AI weekly study plan**: a 7-day schedule generated by Google Gemini, with explanations
- **Tracking**: mark study sessions as completed
- **Saved plans**: save, view and delete study plans
- **Admin**: manage user accounts
